Doctor, Lawyer, Indian Chief: A Story of a Family Reunited (Adventures of Oleo Book 2)

Available in: Paperback, eBook
Published: August 15, 2019
Buy on Amazon

An Enchanting Prequel to Noelle’s Ark

A Princess named Elizabeth was born with the proverbial silver spoon in her mouth. Her family hoped, that one day she would marry a rich and handsome prince, and they would all live happily ever after.

…But, instead… her brother Samuel became one of the biggest Hollywood power brokers in history, and Elizabeth got shot in the head by her nephew at her niece’s wedding, and found herself halfway to heaven with an angel named Oleo, who just might be able to help the Princess change her destination.
