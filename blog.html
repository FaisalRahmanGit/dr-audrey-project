Please write proper Heading

August 31, 2019 by Audrey Levy

Hey guys, I am ready for advance readers on “Doctor, Lawyer, Indian Chief!” Who’s interested? I will email you a copy of the Word Document. I’m looking for people who are willing to give me honest reviews in Amazon, to tell me if I have typos, to tell me if I say something that doesn’t make any sense, to tell me if they think that what I’ve written is shit. Now is the time before I put it in print. Is anybody interested?

Oh, I want to add that it’s only 122 pages (a short novel). It’ll take you less than a couple of hours to read it. And this is what it’s about… Once upon a time, a Princess named Elizabeth was born with the proverbial silver spoon in her mouth. Her family hoped that one day she would marry a rich and handsome prince, and they would all live happily ever after…
…But instead… her brother Samuel became one of the biggest Hollywood power brokers in history, and Elizabeth got shot in the head by her nephew at her niece’s wedding, and found herself halfway to heaven with an angel named Oleo, who just might be able to help the Princess change her destination!

Okay – so I need readers to see if I’ve made any glaring mistakes. Free word copies to be emailed to you. In return, I would appreciate an honest review on Amazon on the day that the book launches which will be sometime in September – I’ll keep you posted on that.

Take care,

Audrey
